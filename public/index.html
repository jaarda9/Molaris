AHA Antibiotic Prophylaxis (Single Pre-op Dose)

Standard Adult (Oral):
Amoxicillin 2.0 g (four 500mg capsules)
Take 30 to 60 minutes before dental procedure. (Child: 50 mg/kg)
Penicillin Allergy (Oral):
Cephalexin 2.0 g OR Azithromycin 500 mg
Note: Clindamycin is no longer recommended by AHA due to C. difficile risk.

ADA Post-Op Analgesic Protocol

Mild to Moderate Post-op Pain:

Ibuprofen 400 mg q4-6h prn

Severe Post-op Pain (Staggered Synergistic Combo):

Ibuprofen 600 mg + Acetaminophen 1000 mg every 6 hours

Superior to codeine/opioid combinations with fewer adverse events (Cochrane review).
